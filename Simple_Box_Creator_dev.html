Bottom Type: Grooved Options
Slide-in bottom: grooved into Side 1, Side 2 & End 1 (Box joints only - with Dovetail joints, End 1's groove is skipped, since it would land inside the angled dovetail tails there). End 2 is shortened to the top of the groove, so the panel can always slide in/out there, even after glue-up. Uncheck "Bottom" under Faces To Machine below to keep the groove but skip cutting the panel itself (e.g. to slide in your own material). Also includes Side Overhang: Front & Back (End 1 & End 2) can each get their own height below the box height, tapered by a chamfer - only with Lid Type = None.
